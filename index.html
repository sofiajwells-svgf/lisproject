Why Visit Germany

Beautiful Destinations

Germany has many beautiful sights to see from big cities to small charming towns

Rich Culture and History

Museums, landmarks, and architecture help show Germany's culture

Delicious Food

Enjoy traditional German foods, visit local bakeries, and explore the different flavors and dishes found throughout the country

Featured Destination: Munich

Munich is a city I recommend exploring for its beautiful architecture, lively atmosphere, and Bavarian culture. Munich is a great mix of old history, as well as new modern attractions.

Travel Tips

A little preparation can make your trip easier and more enjoyable. Keep these recommendations in mind when planning your visit.

  • Plan your destinations before traveling
  • Research train routes and transportation
  • Learn a few basic German phrases, a little goes a long way and locals love when you try and speak their language!

Food Recommendations

Trying traditional German food is a great way to experience the country's culture. Here are a few foods I recommend trying during your trip.

  • Bratwurst
  • Schnitzel
  • Pretzels
